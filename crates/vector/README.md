# pizzico-vector

Motore geometrico vettoriale di Pizzico basato su geometria differenziale computazionale e standard aperti.

## Riferimenti Scientifici (§6.2)
- **Bézier parametriche cubiche**: Lyche, T. & Merrien, J. L. *Parametric Bézier Curves and Splines*, Springer 2014.
- **Curvature Tool (κ-curves)**: Miura, N. et al. *κ-curves: Interpolation at local maximum curvature points*, The Visual Computer 2021.
- **GPU-Friendly Stroke Expansion**: Levien, R. & Uguray, A. *High-Performance GPU Stroke Expansion with Euler Spirals*, PACMCGIT 2024.
- **Operazioni Booleane Esatte (Pathfinder)**: Berberich, E. et al. *A Complete, Exact and Efficient Implementation for Computing Arrangements of Conic Arcs*, ESA 2002.
- **Rasterizzazione Differenziabile**: Li, T. M. et al. *Differentiable Vector Graphics Rasterization*, SIGGRAPH 2020.
